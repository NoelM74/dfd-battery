## 1. Who DFD actually is (corrected figures)

- **Legal entity:** Do-Fluoride New Energy Technology Co., Ltd — a subsidiary of **Do-Fluoride New Materials Co., Ltd** (Shenzhen-listed, **SZ002407**), a global leader in lithium hexafluorophosphate (LiPF₆).
- **Founded:** December 2010. **Registered capital: ¥1.66163 billion** (corrects the stale ¥703M LinkedIn figure).
- **Group scale:** **22+ subsidiaries, 7,000+ staff** (per DFD's own site; corrects LinkedIn's 28/5,000).
- **Capacity:** **~8.5 GWh** combined cell production across **Jiaozuo (Henan)** and **Nanning (Guangxi)** — the Guangxi subsidiary (Guangxi New-Fortune New Energy, est. Jul 2021, ¥600M capital) is a dedicated global green-energy/storage arm.
- **Overseas footprint:** production/interest bases in **Indonesia and Bolivia**; **European supplier partnerships** on cathode materials and recycling already underway (localisation is *in motion*, not a cold start).
- **IP / scale proof:** 851 patent applications, 623 authorised patents; contributions to 106 international/national/industry standards; proprietary **bidirectional pressure-relief** safety design.
- **The "1 in 4" halo:** roughly a quarter of the world's lithium batteries use the parent's polyfluoride materials — a rare upstream credibility asset almost no cell competitor can claim.

**The core commercial problem (the reason this project exists):** almost all useful, discoverable information about DFD's cells — specs, MOQs, pricing — currently surfaces through **third-party marketplaces** (Made-in-China, evlithium, Accio, Neexgent) rather than dfdxny.com. The factory-level product is credible and in demand; the **demand capture** (lead, margin, relationship) is being intermediated. That is a revenue leak, not a branding vanity problem.

## 2. Product lines (verified)

**Cells — large-format cylindrical LFP** (the strategic core; DFD's answer to the tabless / large-cylindrical trend CATL/EVE/BYD are racing on):
- Series **33, 40/42, 46, 60/65** + a **prismatic (square aluminium-shell)** format.
- Design signatures: **unilateral confluence** (single-side busbar → lower internal resistance, simpler pack assembly, better pack space utilisation), **full-lug** construction, **dual explosion-proof venting**, top-discharge / bottom heat dissipation.
- Also produces **sodium-ion** ("lithium + sodium battery") alongside LFP.

**Packs — EV & light-electric vehicle:**
- Passenger EV packs running in **SAIC-GM, Chery, NETA** — among the earliest lithium power-battery suppliers to China's A00-class segment, **300,000+ vehicles on the road**.
- Two-wheeler/three-wheeler pack codes: e-bike **361X / 481X**, e-moped **482X / 602X**, e-motorcycle **603X / 723X**, e-trike **60V/72V**, paired with 33/40/42 or 60-series cells.

**Energy Storage Systems — four sub-products:**
1. Industrial & Commercial (C&I) energy-storage power station (outdoor IP54, high-safety long-life system, AC/DC converter, precision liquid cooling, temperature control).
2. Stacked wall-mounted home storage (modular, BMS-managed, expandable).
3. **500W** portable power system.
4. **5,000W** backup power system (modular, smart LED display, protection suite).

**Also:** motors & electronic control systems (EV/e-moto drivetrain), battery **recycling & circular economy** (closed-loop production→use→recycling→regeneration), telecom/microgrid backup.

**Certifications (confirmed, not hypothetical):** **UL1973, UL1642, IEC62619, UN38.3**; Chinese national standards **GB/T 31484-2015** (cycle life), **GB/T 31486-2015** (electrical performance), **GB 38031-2020** (power-battery safety).

## 4. Tariff-adjusted market prioritisation

Global BESS demand is strong everywhere (2026 additions projected >130 GW / ~350 GWh, led by China, US, UK, Australia, Germany; MEA, KSA, Chile, Eastern Europe rising). But **where DFD can sell profitably** is a narrower list.

| Priority | Markets | Why | Trade reality |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Track A — now** | US/EU integrators via **non-China origin** (Indonesia/Bolivia) | Displaced demand actively hunting compliant, politically-defensible non-China supply. | 82% US tariff is **origin-based** — Indonesia-origin sidesteps it and creates a premium SKU. |
| **Track A — now** | **SEA** (Vietnam, Philippines, Indonesia — domestic demand) | Huge e-two-wheeler + residential/C&I ESS volume; existing Indonesia base. | Open. Price-sensitive — use for throughput, not premium. |
| **Track B — build** | **MEA** (Saudi, UAE, Egypt) | $2.85B → $6.9B by 2031, 19% CAGR; Saudi 48 GWh mandate; Egypt 4 GW + 500 MWh+ new storage by late 2026. | No anti-China trade defence — but won via EPC relationships, local content, in-region warranty. **12–24 mo.** |
| **Track B — build** | **EU** (Germany, Netherlands, Italy) | Chinese cell/BESS imports up ~7×; EV tariffs have **not** extended to standalone cells. | Barrier is compliance, not tariff: **Battery Passport** is the 2026 gate. First-mover compliance = moat. |
| **Deprioritise** | **United States (direct China-origin)** | Demand exists but landed cost is uncompetitive. | ~82% total tariff from Jan 1 2026. Serve via non-China origin only; monitor Section 232 talks. |

**Bottom line:** the trade war hands MEA, Africa and SEA to Chinese manufacturers as the path of least resistance, turns the EU into a **compliance race** (not a price race), and — crucially — **the origin lever partially re-opens the West.** That should shape which languages, case studies and certifications the site leads with (EN + AR + DE + VI; MEA and EU compliance case studies front-and-centre).
