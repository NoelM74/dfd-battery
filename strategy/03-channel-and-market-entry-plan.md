## 2. The two tracks

### Track A — revenue now (0–6 months)
- **Wedge:** "Non-China-origin LFP at Chinese-parent quality" (Indonesia/Bolivia). Target US/EU BESS + pack integrators eating the 82% China-origin tariff.
- **Motion:** cold outreach + trade shows → RFQ → data-room verification → sample order → PO.
- **Parallel:** SEA e-two-wheeler + residential ESS volume (price-led, high-throughput).

### Track B — the moat (6–24 months)
- **EU Battery-Passport-ready traceability** → bid-qualifying before rivals.
- **MEA presence:** distributor/EPC relationships + a regional warranty/service entity so Saudi/Egypt mandate deals are winnable.

## 3. The 90-day sequence

**Weeks 1–2 — build the trust layer (do this before any traffic):**
- Consolidate UL1973/UL1642/IEC62619/UN38.3 + GB certs, cell datasheets, and factory footage into one crawlable location; a buyer must verify certs + see the factory in **under 2 minutes.**
- Stand up the data-room pages (Trust & Compliance, one hero-SKU page, Non-China Origin page) with a 3-field RFQ routed to a monitored inbox with a **4-business-hour SLA.**
- Draft named warranty terms and the parent-financial/registration proof block.

**Weeks 2–4 — light the revenue engine:**
- Build a 150–200 account target list of US/EU BESS/pack integrators (Sales Navigator + BNEF/industry directories + prior China-origin buyers).
- Launch cold outreach led by the non-China-origin one-pager (see sales-enablement asset). Sequence: connect → problem (tariff pain) → proof (origin + certs + parent) → soft CTA (spec sheet) → RFQ.
- Book the next available Intersolar/RE+/ESS-MEA slot; prep badge-scan → outreach workflow.

**Weeks 4–12 — convert and compound:**
- Respond to every RFQ within SLA; move qualified leads to sample orders.
- Publish the first GEO/AIO fact-block content + FAQ pages targeting regional buyer-intent keywords.
- Begin MEA distributor conversations and EU passport data-layer scoping in parallel (Track B seeding).

## 4. KPIs (leading, not vanity)

| Metric | Why it matters | Target (first 90 days) |
|---|---|---|
| RFQs generated (by channel) | The only near-term revenue signal | 15–30 qualified RFQs |
| RFQ response time | Beats resellers on the thing buyers feel | ≤ 4 business hours, 100% |
| Cert-PDF downloads / factory-video plays | High-intent verification behaviour | Track & trend up |
| Sample orders → POs | Conversion of trust into revenue | ≥ 3 sample orders |
| GEO citations (Perplexity/ChatGPT naming DFD) | Land-grab progress | First citations by month 3–4 |
| Distributor/EPC conversations opened (MEA) | Track B pipeline | ≥ 5 |

Vanity metrics to **ignore** early: raw organic traffic, keyword rankings for head terms, Alibaba impressions. They do not equal RFQs.

## 6. Compliance-as-marketing

Turn the EU Battery Passport and safety certifications into **content assets** (whitepapers, readiness briefs, case studies) that are simultaneously (a) sales collateral for procurement risk committees and (b) GEO/AIO citation fuel. This is the highest-leverage content DFD can produce because it serves the trust job and the ranking job at once.

## 7. Guardrails
- Validate the exact tariff stack and any tender figures with a customs broker / local counsel before quoting them to a buyer.
- Do not over-promise MEA timelines — those are relationship sales, not inbound.
- Keep entity data (name, registration, parent) identical across all channels for GEO entity resolution.
