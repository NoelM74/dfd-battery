# DFD New Energy — International Expansion Strategy
### Executive Summary · The Plan to Success

**Prepared:** July 2026 · **For:** DFD New Energy Technology Co., Ltd (a Do-Fluoride Chemicals Group company, SZ002407)
**Objective:** Grow B2B sales outside China and pull leads + margin back from the resellers currently intermediating the demand.

---

## 1. The one-paragraph thesis

DFD makes a genuinely competitive, certified, listed-parent-backed product — but on the international stage the **reseller is the brand**. Alibaba and Made-in-China listings outrank dfdxny.com, so the lead, the margin, and the customer relationship all leak to middlemen. The fix is **not** "better SEO to rank above the resellers." It is to make DFD the only party in the transaction that can do what a reseller structurally cannot: **originate a bankable warranty, put a public company's balance sheet behind it, and prove it.** Everything below flows from that. The website's job is to be a **due-diligence data room** that converts the high-intent buyers who are already checking DFD out — and to become the **verifiable answer** that both Google and AI answer-engines cite. Revenue in the next 12 months comes from **two tracks run in parallel**, not from one hero market.

| Signal | Implication |

## 3. The strategy (validated by LLM council pressure-test)

**Run a two-track go-to-market. Fix trust-legibility before spending a dollar on traffic.**

### Track A — Revenue now (0–6 months)
- **Lead wedge: "non-China origin."** Target US/EU BESS + battery-pack integrators who bought China-origin cells and now eat the tariff. DFD's Indonesia/Bolivia output gives them a compliant, cost-sane, politically-defensible alternative **no reseller can offer.** This is the sharpest, most under-exploited angle in the whole plan.
- **Engine: cold outreach + targeted trade shows** (Intersolar/ees Europe, RE+, Energy Storage Summit, a LatAm energy expo leveraging the Bolivia base). These produce real RFQs in 90 days; SEO does not.
- **SEA volume in parallel** for the e-two-wheeler and residential ESS lines.

### Track B — The moat (6–24 months)
- **EU Battery-Passport-ready traceability** built now → bid-qualifying before rivals.
- **MEA presence-building**: distributor/EPC relationships + a regional warranty/service entity so the mandate deals are winnable when they land.

### Channel mix (roles, not a menu)
- **Owned website** → due-diligence **data room** + **GEO/AIO citation layer**. The compounding asset. Not the 90-day revenue engine.
- **Cold outreach + trade shows** → the near-term revenue engine.
- **Alibaba/Made-in-China** → keep as a trust-scaffold and discovery surface; route qualified buyers to direct.
- **Distributors/agents** → the MEA + warranty-presence unlock, medium-term.

## 4. The #1 thing that must be true

> **DFD must become the verifiable, contractable entity** — certifications mapped to real production batches, a factory-audit video, company-registration and parent-financial proof, named warranty terms backed by Do-Fluoride (SZ002407), and a credible path to in-region service.

Without that, a better-ranked website simply does the reseller's lead-generation for free. With it, DFD becomes the safe direct choice **and** the citable source of truth for AI answer engines.

## 5. Do this first

Before any content or ad spend, assemble the **verifiable trust layer**: a crawlable, downloadable certification library (UL1973 / UL1642 / IEC62619 / UN38.3 + GB standards) mapped to batches, a 2-minute factory-audit video, parent-financial/registration proof, and named warranty terms. This is the credibility gate resellers cannot fake, the precondition for GEO citation, and the asset every channel points back to.
