# DFD New Energy — International Expansion Package

A research-backed go-to-market strategy plus working demos (website + slide deck) to help **DFD New Energy** (dfdxny.com — a Do-Fluoride Chemicals Group company, SZ002407) grow B2B battery sales **outside China** and reclaim leads + margin from the resellers currently intermediating its demand.

## The one-line thesis
On the international stage, the **reseller is the brand**. The fix isn't "better SEO to outrank Alibaba" — it's making DFD the only party in the deal that can **originate a bankable, listed-parent-backed warranty and prove it.** The website becomes a **due-diligence data room**; revenue comes from a **two-track** GTM led by an under-priced **non-China-origin** (Indonesia/Bolivia) wedge.

## The strategy in five lines
1. **Fix trust-legibility first** — certs mapped to batches, factory-audit video, parent-backed warranty, named references. The credibility gate resellers can't fake.
2. **Track A (0–6 mo revenue):** cold outreach + trade shows to tariff-displaced US/EU integrators, led by the **non-China-origin** story; SEA volume in parallel.
3. **Track B (6–24 mo moat):** EU Battery-Passport-ready traceability + MEA distributor/warranty presence.
4. **Website = data room + GEO/AIO citation layer.** Compounding, not the 90-day engine.
5. **The #1 must-be-true:** DFD becomes the verifiable, contractable entity — or a better website just does the reseller's lead-gen for free.

## Notes on data integrity
Company facts are reconciled from DFD's own site, Alibaba storefront and page screenshots (high confidence). Market sizes, CAGRs and tariff figures are directional industry/trade data — **validate the exact tariff stack and tender figures with a customs broker before quoting them in a contract.** No search-volume numbers are fabricated; keyword clusters must be run through Ahrefs/SEMrush/GKP before locking a content calendar.
