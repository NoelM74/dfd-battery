Who we are

A manufacturer with an upstream advantage almost no cell-maker can claim.

Founded in December 2010, DFD New Energy engineers and manufactures new-energy power batteries, materials, modules and automotive battery packs. What makes us different sits one level up: our parent, Do-Fluoride, makes the lithium hexafluorophosphate at the heart of the modern lithium battery — so roughly one in four of the world's lithium batteries already runs on our group's chemistry.
