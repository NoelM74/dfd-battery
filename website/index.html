Product Range

Six product lines. One factory relationship.

Cells, packs, storage systems, and control electronics — sourced from a single manufacturer instead of stitched together across brokers.

Cylindrical LFP Cells

Large-format 33, 40/42, 46, and 60/65 series cylindrical cells, plus a prismatic (square) format. Dual explosion-proof venting, full-lug construction, top-discharge / bottom heat dissipation.

Cycle life: 4,000+ @ 70% SOH · 2C max continuous

Passenger & Light-EV Packs

Passenger EV packs running in SAIC-GM, Chery, and NETA models; e-bike, moped, motorcycle and tricycle packs across the 33/40/42/60 series.

Pack codes: 361X · 482X · 603X · 723X
