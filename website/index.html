Flagship platform

Large-Format Cylindrical LFP Cells

Four cylindrical series, plus prismatic and sodium-ion. Our unilateral-confluence design brings both terminals to one face of the cell: lower connection resistance, one busbar plane instead of two, and reclaimed volume in every pack you build.

Nominal 3.2 V · 4,000 cycles to 70% SOH (GB/T 31484) · Dual venting

EV & E-Mobility Packs

Passenger EV packs already running in SAIC-GM, Chery and NETA vehicles, plus e-bike, moped, motorcycle and tricycle platforms across the 33/40/42/60 series.

Pack codes 361X · 482X · 603X · 723X

Energy Storage Systems

One cell and one BMS family across four system sizes: IP54 liquid-cooled C&I cabinets, stacked wall-mounted home storage, and portable and backup units.
