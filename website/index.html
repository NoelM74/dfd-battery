Large-Format Cylindrical LFP Cells

The 33, 40/42, 46 and 60/65 series — plus a prismatic format and sodium-ion. Our unilateral-confluence design puts the busbar on one side: lower internal resistance, simpler pack assembly, more usable space inside every pack.

Nominal 3.2 V · Cycle life 4,000+ @ 70% SOH · Dual explosion-proof venting

EV & E-Mobility Packs

Passenger EV packs proven in SAIC-GM, Chery and NETA vehicles. Plus e-bike, moped, motorcycle and tricycle packs across the 33/40/42/60 series.
