The problem

Right now, the reseller is the brand.

Alibaba and Made-in-China listings outrank dfdxny.com. So the lead, the margin, and the customer relationship all leak to middlemen — for a product DFD actually makes.

  • Almost every discoverable spec, MOQ and price lives on a reseller's listing, not DFD's own domain.
  • The demand exists and is being captured by intermediaries — a revenue leak, not a vanity branding gap.
  • Buyers never build a relationship with DFD — they build it with the trader who can service them.
Who DFD actually is

A listed-parent-backed manufacturer — not a trading house.

Scale & backing

Subsidiary of Do-Fluoride New Materials (SZ002407). 22+ subsidiaries, 7,000+ staff, 8.5 GWh across Jiaozuo & Nanning. ~1 in 4 of the world's lithium batteries use the parent's materials.

Proven & certified

UL1973 · UL1642 · IEC62619 · UN38.3 + GB standards. 300,000+ vehicles with SAIC-GM, Chery, NETA. 623 authorised patents. Overseas bases in Indonesia & Bolivia.

None of this is currently legible to an international buyer. That is the whole opportunity.

The core insight

B2B battery buyers don't buy amp-hours.
They buy risk transfer.

A procurement engineer is buying a warranty that's still honourable in 2034, supply security, and a supplier name their own risk committee can defend without getting fired.

  • A reseller can copy the public cert and the spec sheet.
  • A reseller cannot originate a bankable warranty, put a listed balance sheet behind it, or prove batch-level traceability.
  • That gap is DFD's entire moat — the website's job is to make it legible and provable.

The strategy · validated by LLM council

Two tracks, run in parallel.

Cold outreach + trade shows to tariff-displaced US/EU integrators, led by the non-China-origin wedge. SEA e-two-wheeler + residential ESS volume in parallel. Produces real RFQs in 90 days.

Track B — the moat (6–24 mo)

The owned channel

The website is a data room, not a brochure.

Its job is to convert the high-intent buyers already checking DFD out — and to become the answer AI engines cite.

  • Verifiable proof: certs mapped to batches, factory-audit video, parent-backed warranty, named references.
  • GEO / AIO: structured fact-blocks + schema so ChatGPT & Perplexity cite DFD — not a reseller — for "who makes this cell."
  • Mobile-first, multilingual (EN/AR/DE/VI), Core-Web-Vitals-tuned, RFQ with a 4-hour response SLA.
